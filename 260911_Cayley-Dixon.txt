The `p`-adic step now makes the dependency question sharper—what additional structure is actually required beyond `InvolutionAlgebra` + prime `p` to define the norm, valuation, and completion without quietly reintroducing Archimedean assumptions?

So: what is the minimum additional leading specification needed for the next trailing generalization?

If \(S_n\) denotes the specification at a reading point, then locally:

\[
S_{n-1}\longrightarrow\boxed{S_n}\longrightarrow S_{n+1}.
\]

The same additional step can be read from its two sides:

\[
S_n\xrightarrow{+1}S_{n+1},
\]

where \(S_n\) leads \(S_{n+1}\), while \(S_{n+1}\) trails \(S_n\).

For this case, schematically:

\[
\begin{aligned}
S_0 &= \text{Cayley--Dickson construction} \\
&\longrightarrow S_1 = \texttt{InvolutionAlgebra} \\
&\longrightarrow S_2 = \text{\(p\)-adic extension}.
\end{aligned}
\]

So the practical question is: what is the minimum \(+1\) specification required for \(S_1\to S_2\)?
